# Code of conduct

People are welcome to participate regardless of background or experience. English and Chinese contributions are both welcome. Discuss the work with patience, use concrete evidence, and make room for questions and disagreement.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share private information without consent, impersonate others or repeatedly disrupt a discussion. Critique a proposal or behavior without attacking the person. Do not use issues, reviews or discussions for unsolicited promotion or repeated demands for an immediate response.

## Reporting and moderation

For a conduct concern, use a private contact route documented by the target repository when one is available. If none is listed, a minimal public request for a private contact is acceptable; omit names, sensitive details and evidence from that request. Use GitHub's **Report content** or abuse-reporting tools for platform abuse or threats. Security vulnerabilities follow [the security policy](https://github.com/tangyixiao/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may ask participants to change course, edit or remove content, close a conversation, or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports are handled with discretion, without a guaranteed response deadline or promise of absolute confidentiality. Participants may provide context and ask the maintainer to reconsider a moderation decision through an available private route.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces the maintainer explicitly operates for the project. It does not create an organization, committee or support service.
